Menova: screen proposals for owner approval (P0-08)

For each screen: the live screen captured on 2026-11-20 (headless Chrome, 375 × 812 phone, or 1280 px desktop; the cart was pre-filled through localStorage, analytics requests were blocked, and nothing was submitted), the proposed design built with the approved tokens from app_theme.dart (colours, 18/14 px radii, pill buttons, Roboto), and what changes and why. Real menu data from tools/seed: Supreme (EGP 175, Medium +60, Stuffed crust +45, Pepperoni +35 each, Ranch sauce +15) and Nutella & banana crepe (EGP 95, Caramel sauce +15).

Limits kept on purpose: cash only (no online payment), no ratings, no driver tracking, no new data. Everything shown already exists in the cart, Order or OrderItem snapshots. All buttons are 44 px or taller.

What changes and why (checkout)
  • Three titled sections (details, how, your order) instead of one long list of fields, with the approved section style (icon + bold title).
  • Pick up / Delivery as two large cards with a one-line hint, replacing the small Material segmented control; delivery reveals address, location button and the priced result (“4.6 km by road · Delivery fee EGP 30”) in a tinted info box, errors in red as today.
  • Order summary on the page: the customer confirms what they are paying for (the lines, from the cart) next to the totals; coupon and notes stay.
  • The button carries the total (“Place order · EGP 615”) and keeps today's states: sending (locked), checking, waiting to send + Try again. Price-change dialog unchanged.
What changes and why (confirmation)
  • Clear success header and a large order number, both from order.id (short reference).
  • Order status and payment shown separately as two cards; payment adds “Cash on delivery / at pickup” from the fulfillment.
  • Horizontal stepper instead of a tall list; pickup skips “On the way” (already the logic) and ends with “Picked up”.
  • What was ordered, delivery address and the frozen totals, all read from the order snapshot (no recalculation).
  • Top bar and “Back to Menu”, so the page is not a dead end.

What changes and why (board and card)
  • Columns by status (new, accepted, preparing, ready / on the way) instead of one mixed grid: the kitchen sees what to start next at a glance. Same live query and the same activeStatuses, grouped on the client; on a phone the columns become tabs with counts.
  • Cards grow with their content (no fixed 520 px height with a hidden inner scroll that can hide a “بدون” line).
  • “بدون X” as a bordered red block, extras as bold green “+” lines, kept defaults small and muted, notes in a yellow box: the three things the kitchen must not miss are styled differently from each other.
  • Header shows ref, pickup/delivery tag, payment tag and the age of the order (from createdAt); new orders keep the red outline + sound. A failed price or delivery check gets a red border and the existing red message at the top of the card, before the buttons.
  • Full-width 44 px actions: the next allowed status (from nextStatuses), cancel in outline red, print; payment actions as a lighter second row so they are not confused with status.
  • Reception uses the storefront tokens (copied values) instead of the default Material seed colour, so both sites look like one brand.
What changes and why (ticket)
  • Order number larger (30 px) and pickup/delivery as an inverted black band, readable from the pass.
  • “بدون X” keeps the thick box and gains a ✕ mark; extras stay bold; notes move into a dashed box.
  • Itemised totals and a boxed payment line (“غير مدفوع · نقداً”) so the driver or cashier knows what to collect. Same 302 px width and frozen data.

Buildability: all proposals use widgets already in the apps (Card/Container, Chip, FilledButton/OutlinedButton/TextButton, ListView/GridView or Row of columns, TabBar, the existing TopBar) and data already in CartLineView, Order and OrderItem. New strings go to AppStrings in both languages (storefront) or Arabic (reception). Screenshots are in this folder (current_*.png); emoji icons in the mockups stand for Material icons.
